IBITBEST Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026 · Version 1.0

Overview

IBITBEST is a casual game hub with four mini games: Pan & Tilt, Lighthouse Beam, Glove Catch, and Peg Solitaire. This policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app does not include advertising identifiers or analytics trackers operated by us.

Information stored on your device

The app may store the following locally to support gameplay and preferences:

  • Game progress, scores, and unlock levels for each mini game
  • Settings such as haptic feedback and calm pace for Glove Catch
  • Tutorial completion flags
  • Session and streak counters used only for in-app stats display

This information remains on your device and is used solely to run IBITBEST. Removing the app deletes this stored data.

Children

IBITBEST is designed as a family-friendly casual experience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about this app, please contact us using the support channel listed on the App Store product page.

Third parties

IBITBEST does not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. Apple may process purchase and device information according to Apple’s own policies when you download or update the app from the App Store.

Security

We design the app to keep progress on your device. No security measure is perfect; you are responsible for keeping your device secure with a passcode or biometric lock if you choose to use one.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

For privacy questions about IBITBEST, use the support contact provided on the App Store listing for this app.
